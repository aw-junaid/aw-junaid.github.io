Tracking Technologies and Cookies

We utilize Cookies and similar tracking technologies to monitor and analyze activity on Our Service, and to store certain information that enhances Your experience. These tracking technologies, which include beacons, tags, and scripts, are employed to collect and track information, as well as to improve and analyze the performance and functionality of Our Service. The specific types of technologies We use may include:

  • Cookies or Browser Cookies: A cookie is a small text file placed on Your Device by a website. You have the ability to instruct Your browser to refuse all Cookies or to indicate when a Cookie is being sent, providing You with control over their use. However, please be aware that if You do not accept Cookies, You may not be able to use some parts or functionalities of our Service effectively.   • Web Beacons: Certain sections of our Service and our email communications may contain small electronic files known as web beacons (also referred to as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These tiny, invisible graphics permit the Company, for example, to count users who have visited particular pages or opened an email. They are also used for other related website statistics, such as recording the popularity of a certain section, verifying system and server integrity, and understanding user engagement with email campaigns.

Cookies can be broadly categorized based on their duration:

  • Persistent Cookies: These Cookies remain on Your personal computer or mobile device even after You go offline. They are used to remember Your preferences or actions across multiple sessions, such as staying logged in or recalling site settings.
  • Session Cookies: These Cookies are temporary and are deleted as soon as You close Your web browser. They are typically used for essential functions during a single browsing session, such as managing a shopping cart or maintaining login status for the duration of Your visit.
