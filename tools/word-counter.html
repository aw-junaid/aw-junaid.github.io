✦ How This Tool Works

How is "word count" calculated?

Text is split on whitespace and any chunk containing at least one letter or number counts as a word. Standalone punctuation is ignored.

How is reading/speaking time estimated?

Reading time assumes ~225 words per minute. Speaking time assumes ~130 words per minute, both typical averages for adult readers/speakers.

Does it understand Markdown?

Yes — it detects headings, list items, links, inline/fenced code, and bold/italic markers, so you can sanity-check a raw .md draft before publishing.

Is my text stored anywhere?

Your draft is auto-saved only to your own browser's local storage so a refresh doesn't lose your work. It's never uploaded, logged, or sent to a server, and clearing it removes it for good.

How is the readability score calculated?

It uses the Flesch Reading Ease formula based on words, sentences, and estimated syllables, plus a rough Flesch–Kincaid grade level. Treat both as estimates, not exact scores.

What do the Text Tools do?

They rewrite your text in place: change case, strip extra spaces and blank lines, remove duplicate or sort lines, and reverse the text — all undoable with Ctrl/Cmd+Z since they edit the textarea directly.

Can I see stats for just part of my text?

Yes — select (highlight) any text in the editor and a note under the toolbar will show the word and character count for just that selection, and Text Tools will act on that selection only.

How does Find & Replace work?

Find jumps to the first match; Next/Prev step through all matches, wrapping around; Replace swaps just the currently selected match and advances; Replace All swaps every match at once. Toggle "Match case" or "Whole word" to narrow results.
