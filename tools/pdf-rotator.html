✦ How This Tool Works

Which rotation mode should I use?

Uniform rotates the whole document by one angle — the everyday case for a whole file scanned sideways. Odd/Even fixes a duplex scan where the back sides came out upside-down. Page Ranges handles a document with a few sections in different orientations. Individual Pages is for a handful of one-off pages that need a specific fix.

What does "absolute" vs "relative" rotation mean?

Absolute sets the final orientation regardless of what the page's rotation metadata currently says — safest when you're not sure of the source state. Relative adds your chosen angle on top of whatever rotation the page already has, useful when a file is already partly rotated and just needs a further nudge.

What does Auto-Straighten actually check?

After your rotation rules run, it looks at each page's sheet dimensions and adds an extra 90° to any page still wider than it is tall. It's built for scanner batches that mix upright and sideways sheets, so you don't have to hunt down which pages are the odd ones out.

How do I write a page range or override list?

Ranges use comma-separated numbers and dashes, like 1-3,5,8-10. Individual-page overrides use page:angle pairs, like 3:90, 7:180. Both are 1-indexed and clamp automatically to the source file's real page count.

Will page numbers and watermarks respect the new orientation?

Yes — both are drawn after rotation is applied, so a page number stamped "bottom center" reads correctly against the page's final orientation rather than its original one.

Is anything uploaded to a server?

No. The file you add is read and rotated locally in your browser — nothing is sent anywhere. Reload the page and everything is cleared, except your settings, which are remembered for next time.
