A note on legal validity

Electronic signatures are legally recognized in many jurisdictions (e.g. under the US ESIGN Act/UETA or the EU eIDAS regulation), but requirements vary by country, document type, and use case. This tool embeds a visual signature and an optional certificate/hash record for your own audit trail — it does not issue a qualified digital certificate. For contracts, deeds, or regulated filings, confirm what your jurisdiction and counterparty require.

✦ How This Tool Works

How do I place my signature exactly where I want it?

Create your signature (draw, type, or upload), then press "Place Signature" to arm it and click anywhere on the page preview — that's where it lands. Fine-tune the exact X/Y position, width, rotation, and opacity afterward in the "Placed Items" tab, or turn on multi-place mode to drop several copies without re-arming.

Can I sign a 40-page contract without clicking 40 times?

Yes — "Sign every page" or "Initial every page" adds one instance of the item to every page automatically at the corner you choose in Placement Defaults, which is the common real-world need for initialing each page of a long agreement.

What does the signature certificate page actually prove?

It lists the signer's name, stated reason and location, the exact timestamp, and a SHA-256 hash computed from the original file before any signature was applied. Anyone can recompute that hash from the original to confirm it matches, giving a simple integrity record — not a substitute for a qualified digital certificate authority.

My uploaded signature photo has a white background — will that show as a box?

Turn on "Remove white background" before using it. The tool walks every pixel and makes near-white ones transparent based on the whiteness threshold you set, so a phone photo of an ink signature on paper drops onto the page cleanly.

What's the difference between a stamp and a signature?

A signature/initials item is meant to represent your identity on the document. A stamp (CONFIDENTIAL, APPROVED, DRAFT, VOID, COPY, or custom text) is a status marker, typically placed diagonally with some transparency across a page — useful for routing and review workflows rather than signing.

Is my document or signature ever uploaded anywhere?

No. The PDF is rendered and signed entirely in your browser using pdf.js and pdf-lib; nothing is sent to a server. Saved signatures are kept only in this browser's local storage so you can reuse them next time — reload on a different device and you'll need to recreate them.
