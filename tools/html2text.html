✦ How This Tool Works

How are links and images handled?

Choose whether a link keeps just its visible text, appends the URL in parentheses, becomes Markdown syntax, or turns into a numbered footnote with a reference list appended to the output. Images can keep their alt text, be wrapped as "[IMG: alt text]", or be dropped entirely.

How are tables converted?

Tables can be flattened to neatly space-aligned columns for readability, Markdown-style pipe tables for docs, tab-separated rows for pasting into a spreadsheet, or skipped altogether if you only want prose.

What does "clean up leftover entities" fix?

Some CMS exports double-encode text, so after normal parsing you're left with literal text like "&amp;" or "&nbsp;" instead of the character it should be. This option runs a second pass to resolve the common ones so your output doesn't carry stray markup.

What's the Stats tab for?

It reports word and character counts, estimated reading time, and how many links, images, headings and tables were found — useful for checking a meta description length or estimating how long an article takes to read.

Can I convert multiple files at once?

Yes — upload or drag in several .html files and each becomes a tab above the editor. "Download All (.zip)" converts every loaded file with your current settings and bundles the resulting .txt files into one archive.

Is anything uploaded to a server?

No. Files you upload are read locally in your browser and the conversion happens entirely client-side — nothing is sent anywhere. Your draft and settings only persist in this browser's local storage.
