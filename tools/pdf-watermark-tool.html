Why watermark before you send it

A draft that leaves your hands unmarked is indistinguishable from a final. Watermarking every page — not just the cover — with "Draft", "Confidential", or a client name discourages redistribution, proves provenance if a copy leaks, and is standard practice before sharing contracts, pricing sheets, screenplays, or pre-release designs with anyone outside your organization.

✦ How This Tool Works

Text watermark or logo — can I use both?

Yes. Turn on both the Text Watermark and Logo/Image Watermark sections and each is stamped independently using its own position, opacity, and page-range rules — useful for combining a "Confidential" label with a company logo.

What does "Tiled" layout actually do?

Instead of one watermark per page, the text or logo repeats in a grid across the entire page at your chosen horizontal and vertical spacing, so cropping or covering one instance still leaves the mark elsewhere on the page — the standard approach for anti-leak document stamping.

Can I watermark only specific pages?

Page Selection supports all pages, a custom range like "1-3,5,8-10", odd/even pages only, the first page only, or every page except the first — handy for skipping a cover page or only marking an exhibit section of a contract.

Why add page numbers here instead of separately?

Watermarking and pagination are usually done together before a document goes out for review — stamping "Page X of Y" alongside your watermark in one pass avoids running the file through two separate tools and re-uploading it.

Does the preview match the final output exactly?

The preview renders page 1 of the actual watermarked PDF bytes, not a mock-up, so opacity, rotation, color, and position are exactly what you'll get — check it before applying to a full batch of files.

Is anything uploaded to a server?

No. Every PDF you load is watermarked entirely in your browser using pdf-lib — nothing is sent anywhere, which matters when the files are unreleased contracts, drafts, or pricing documents. Reload the page and everything is cleared except your settings.
