✦ How This Tool Works

How do I reorder pages?

Drag any thumbnail and drop it where you want it — the grid position is the new page order. On touch devices, use a long-press drag. For big rearrangements, use the "Bulk Range Reorder" box instead: type something like 1,3-5,2,rest and click Apply Range.

What does "Delete Selected" actually do?

It marks pages for removal — they're greyed out but stay in the grid so you can undo. Nothing is permanently gone until you click Export. If "Drop deleted pages on export" is unchecked, marked pages are kept in the output instead of removed.

How does bulk rotate work?

Check the boxes on the pages you want to rotate, then click "Rotate Selected" — each click adds another 90° on top of whatever rotation that page already has. Rotation is per-page, so you can mix portrait and landscape pages freely.

What's Odd Only / Even Only for?

Common for duplex-scanned documents: scan one side, get all odd pages; flip the stack, scan the other side, get all even pages (usually in reverse). Use these buttons to isolate one set, export it, then interleave separately if needed.

Can I duplicate a page instead of deleting one?

Yes — select any pages and click "Duplicate Selected". A copy of each is inserted immediately after the original, marked with a green "DUP" badge, and can be dragged, rotated, or deleted independently of its source.

Is anything uploaded to a server?

No. The PDF is read, rendered, and reassembled entirely in your browser using pdf.js and pdf-lib — nothing is sent anywhere. Reload the page and the document is cleared, except your settings, which are remembered for next time.
