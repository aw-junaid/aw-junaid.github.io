✦ How This Tool Works

What's the difference between the two input modes?

"HTML Source" converts raw markup you type, paste, or upload as-is. "Paste Rich Content" lets you paste formatted text copied from a webpage or Word doc — your browser's own HTML is read and converted automatically.

What does "GFM tables & strikethrough" change?

With it on, <table> elements convert to piped Markdown tables and <del>/<s> convert to ~~strikethrough~~, matching GitHub's Markdown flavor.

Why strip scripts and styles?

Script, style, and comment nodes have no Markdown equivalent and would otherwise leak stray text into the output — removing them keeps the result clean by default.

Is anything uploaded to a server?

No. Files and pasted content are processed entirely in your browser — nothing is sent anywhere.
